## The brief

Low Tide releases ambient and minimal records, about forty a year. There was no budget for forty designers and a strong dislike of templates.

## The cut

A system that turns each record's audio into a printed tide. The waveform sets the height of the halftone waves and the key of the record sets the colour of the sun. *No two covers match, and all of them belong.*

## The system

A small tool the label runs on its own: drop in a master file, choose nothing, and export print-ready artwork for sleeve, label and streaming in one go. Typography is locked. Everything else listens.

## Afterwards

Three years and 117 covers later, the tool has never needed a designer. That was the point.
