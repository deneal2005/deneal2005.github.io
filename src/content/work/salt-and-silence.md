## The brief

Salt & Silence makes six fragrances and publishes no notes, no pyramids and no adjectives. Their stockists kept adding them anyway. The founders wanted a direct channel that could sell without describing.

## The cut

Each scent became one photograph of still water and one sentence that is never about the smell. The product page is quiet on purpose: an image, a line, a price, a bag. The water responds to scroll speed. Scroll fast and the surface breaks; slow down and it settles. *The page rewards the pace the product asks for.*

## The system

A headless shop with a custom front-end, a motion system limited to exactly three easings, and packaging typography matched to the site to the half-point. No pop-ups, no countdowns, no "you may also like".

## Afterwards

The sample set, the page that says the least, became the shop's best seller.
