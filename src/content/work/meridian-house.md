## The brief

Meridian House designs low, deep buildings that are mostly about where the light falls. Their old identity was a wordmark in a box. Their website was a slideshow of renders. They wanted something that felt like walking into one of their rooms at the right hour.

## The cut

The identity has no logo in the usual sense. The mark is a *position of the sun*. Every page of the site and every printed piece places a single vermilion disc where the sun actually is above the studio at that moment. Morning proposals carry a low eastern sun; the evening newsletter, a low western one.

## The system

A twelve-column grid taken from their own façade module of 600 mm. One serif, one grotesk, nothing else. Photography is shot in natural light only and never retouched for sky. The website calculates the solar position on the visitor's device and moves the disc accordingly, so the homepage at noon and the homepage at dusk are different compositions.

## Afterwards

The studio now briefs its photographers with a sun-path diagram instead of a shot list.
