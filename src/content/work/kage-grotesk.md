## The brief

Self-initiated. I wanted a display face for my own work that came from something physical rather than from other typefaces.

## The cut

Each glyph started as a paper cut-out photographed under a single lamp at a low angle. *The shadows, not the cut-outs, became the outlines.* The angle of the lamp is a variable axis, so in Kage you can move the sun.

## The system

Three axes (weight, light angle and shadow length) across 612 glyphs. The specimen site puts the light under your cursor, so the letters lean away from you as you read them. Hinting was done by hand for the sizes people actually use.

## Afterwards

Licensed by eleven studios in its first year, including two that used it for things I'd never have dared to.
