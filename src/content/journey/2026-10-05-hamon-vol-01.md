## What I built

A portfolio laid out like a book: a prologue, chapters, a contents page with page numbers measured from the live layout, and a colophon. The images are plates generated from code at build time, and the repositories and activity in the journey come from the GitHub API whenever the site is built.

## What changed along the way

The first draft used a made-up studio and placeholder projects to test the design. This version keeps the design and throws the fiction out: the projects are my public repositories, the dates are their dates, and anything that isn’t known yet stays hidden rather than being filled in.
